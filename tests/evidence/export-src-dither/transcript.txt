## What it establishes

- the group is **registered** (`control.commands_list` count 77, all three ids present
  with their arg/result schemas);
- the **defaults** are dither off and `src_quality: "linear"`;
- a setter **reports the previous value** and records an A16 transaction whose
  `class` is `true_inverse` and `reversible` true — stamped by the registry from
  `ControlReversibilityTable.cpp`, not by the handler;
- **`control.undo` reverses it** (`undone_command: "export.set_dither"`, via
  `lmms::ProjectJournal`, the same stack the GUI's Ctrl+Z unwinds) and the
  previous value is restored;
- a bad value is **refused typed** (`invalid_args`, naming the four wire names)
  and an unknown id answers `not_found` — the closed error-kind set, not a
  silent no-op.
