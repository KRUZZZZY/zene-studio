# Q050-141 - Touch-first layout and large-target mode (P1, L, L13)

Branch `feat/050-Q050-141-oc`, based on `050/m0-train-8` (`2da2ed356`).

## The unit

Goal: a musician enables accessible mode, restarts, and then works the arranger, note editor and
transport with a finger. The unit treats the existing Accessible Mode as the one touch-oriented
setting (owner UX decision: one global zoom plus accessible mode, no separate density preset) and
makes it hold a documented touch minimum: **44 x 44 Qt logical pixels per control target, with at
least 8 logical pixels between adjacent targets**. It also makes one finger act as the left mouse
button where a widget accepted touch, because the arranger accepts touch for pinch-to-zoom and a
tap otherwise reached no clip.

Out of scope, as the unit brief records: a separate touch-density setting, a tablet/mobile
application, multi-touch performance surfaces, patcher gestures, and a launcher/mixer redesign.

## What was built

- `include/Appearance.h` - `accessibleTargetPixels` (44) and `accessibleTargetSpacing` (8) are the
  documented minimum; `accessibleTargetFactor` is derived from them (24 px -> 44 px, 1.833...)
  instead of the former 24 -> 32. Accessible mode is still the one preference in `ui/density`
  (`accessible`/`standard`; legacy `comfortable` and `touch` remain aliases), still read at the
  next start, still written by the unchanged `window.appearance` command.
- `include/DesignTokens.h`, `src/gui/shell/DesignTokens.cpp` - `tokens::targetSize()` is 44 in
  accessible mode and `tokens::targetSpacing(standard)` is at least the 8 px touch gap there.
  `DesignTokensTest` (standard mode) still sees 24; `WorkspaceAccessTest` now measures the 44.
- `src/gui/shell/ShellChrome.cpp` - the shell header's target row and the footer's switches use
  `targetSpacing`, so adjacent transport/panel targets keep the gap in accessible mode.
  `ShellButton` sizes every button from `tokens::targetSize()`.
- `src/gui/noteeditor/NoteEditorToolbar.cpp`, `src/gui/noteeditor/NoteCanvas.cpp`,
  `src/gui/noteeditor/NoteGrid.cpp` - the toolbar row uses the touch gap, the Grid and lane fields
  have at least `targetSize()` height. Accessible note rows are 52 px (a 44 px note centred in its
  row, 8 px to the next row's note) and `NoteGrid::setMinRowHeight` makes 52 px the floor of the
  vertical zoom. A note keeps its musical (drawn) width; `NoteCanvas::touchRect` widens it to a
  44 px-wide touch target for hit-testing only, and `noteAt` tests the drawn rectangles first, then
  the touch targets (nearest note centre wins), so two adjacent short notes never take each other's tap.
- `include/TrackView.h`, `src/gui/tracks/TrackView.cpp`, `TrackContentWidget.cpp`,
  `TrackOperationsWidget.cpp`, `TrackLabelButton.cpp`, `InstrumentTrackView.cpp`,
  `SampleTrackView.cpp`, `AutomationTrackView.cpp`, `PatternTrackView.cpp` - in accessible mode the
  arranger lane is 52 px (a 44 px clip plus the 8 px row gap), clips are at least 44 x 44 (a short
  clip is drawn as a wide-enough target), and the per-track operation buttons (Actions, Mute,
  Solo, Arm) are 44 x 44 with 8 px gaps. The touch column widths follow `trackOperationsWidth()`,
  `trackSettingsWidth()` and the row height follows `trackLaneHeight()`, used by the arranger and
  pattern editor; standard mode keeps the inherited 32 px lane and widths.
- `include/TouchInput.h`, `src/gui/TouchInput.cpp` - `touch::installSingleTouch(w)`: a one-finger
  touch begin/move/end is delivered as the left mouse button to the deepest visible child under
  the finger (the implicit grab a mouse press has). The QTouchEvent point-list access is behind a
  Qt 5.15/Qt 6 compatibility helper (`touchPoints()` and `TouchPoint`), so the file compiles against
  both APIs. Two or more fingers are passed to Qt's gesture recognisers, so the arranger's pinch is
  untouched. Installed on the arranger's content viewport (`src/gui/ArrangerAids.cpp`) - where the
  finger's event actually arrives - on `NoteCanvas` and on every `ShellButton` (transport, header,
  footer): all GUI-thread widget event code, never the audio thread.
- `data/manual/index.html` - the Window and Access sections state the 44 x 44 / 8 px minimum and
  that a finger tap selects a clip or a note and operates Play and Stop.
- `docs/UI-SHELL.md` - test table row for `TouchLayoutTest`.
- No new command and no project-format change: appearance remains user configuration, so old
  projects load and render exactly as before (no save/load path was touched).

## Known limits

- The 44 x 44 minimum governs the transport and shell-header buttons, the note-editor toolbar's
  buttons and fields, note targets, the per-track Actions/Mute/Solo/Arm buttons, and arranger clip
  targets; adjacent targets keep the 8 px touch gap. A very short clip is drawn at a 44 px
  target (wider than its musical length in that one touch mode); a very short note keeps its drawn
  width and is hit through a wider touch target, so overlapping targets resolve to the nearest note.
  Where two notes in one row are closer than 44 px, a tap outside both drawn rectangles takes the
  nearer note; zoom in to separate them.
- The broader workspace's controls (mixer strips, launcher, patcher) are not audited here; Q050-235
  owns that.
- A finger is one mouse button: tap, drag, press/release. Multi-touch beyond the existing pinch
  gestures (for example a two-finger performance surface) is not added here.
- The touch translation is a widget filter, not a platform plugin; a platform that delivers only
  synthesized mouse events already worked and is unaffected.
- No mobile/tablet application, launcher or mixer redesign (Q050-235 owns the broader workspace).
- Accessible mode (this unit) and the touch workspace (Q050-235, `window.appearance {"workspace":"touch"}`)
  are separate preferences after the train-16 integration: the touch workspace sizes the launcher's
  cells, scene and stop buttons through `tokens::tapTargetSize()`; accessible mode sizes the arranger,
  note editor and transport through `tokens::targetSize()` / `tokens::targetSpacing()`. Both hold 44 px.
