# Q050-108 - Per-channel loudness, true-peak and VU metering

Unit: P1, L, area L08 (B-MX-132, B-MX-127, R-mixing-6). Goal: users can choose peak, RMS, VU or LUFS/true-peak metering
per mixer strip, with the shell's loudness readout documented accurately. Batch: `feat/050-Q050-088-oc`.

## What was built

- **Per-strip mode** (`include/StripMeters.h`, `src/core/StripMeters.cpp`): `default` (follows `window.mixer_view`
  `meter_mode` - every older project and new channel), `peak`, `rms`, `pre_fader`, `vu`, `lufs`. Stored on the channel
  (`MixerChannel::m_meterMode`), saved by `mixerstrip::save` as `metermode` only off the default.
- **VU**: two critically damped one-pole stages on the rectified post-fader signal (tau = 300 ms / 6.6384, so a step
  reaches 99 % at 300 ms), scaled so a steady sine reads its peak; the reading in VU is that level against
  `meter.set_reference` (0 VU = the reference dBFS sine, -18 by default). RMS stays its own (one-period) mode.
- **LUFS / true peak**: a `StripLoudness` per strip - the tree's `LufsMeter` on the channel's post-fader signal
  (momentary, short-term, true peak since entering the mode), built on the control thread the first time a strip
  enters `lufs`, published with release/acquire, fed by the mixer worker from a fixed scratch block, read through
  relaxed atomics; a reset is requested by the control thread and done by the worker.
- **Agent**: `mixer.set_strip` gains `meter_mode` (one undo step, `true_inverse` as before); `mixer.strip_get` answers
  `meter_mode` and, under `meters`, `vu_left`/`vu_right`, `vu_reference_dbfs`, `lufs_momentary`, `lufs_short_term`,
  `true_peak_dbtp` (null without a reading).
- **GUI**: the strip's Strip > Meter Mode menu (`mixer.set_strip`); the strip's bars follow its own mode (VU needle,
  momentary loudness as a level) and its meter line reads "VU x.x" or "S x.x LUFS TP y.y".
- **Docs**: `docs/KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md` (the "no meter in the interface" entry corrected: the shell's LUFS field and
  `meter.get_state` are the master's, per-strip modes are separate; the RMS-is-not-VU note updated) and
  `docs/METER-SURFACE.md` §6.

## Commands and format

- Changed: `mixer.set_strip` (`meter_mode`), `mixer.strip_get` (mode and readings). No new ids.
- Format: `<mixerchannel metermode="vu|lufs|...">`, absent = `default`.

## Known limits

- A strip's LUFS has no integrated loudness, LRA or history; it restarts when the strip enters the mode.
- The loudness meter is built at the engine rate the first time a strip asks for it and keeps that rate (the object
  is never replaced while the worker may use it): after an engine rate change its LUFS readings are off (documented, not fixed).
  The VU's integration follows the rate each period.
- The bars still fall by the view's fall-off between readings.
