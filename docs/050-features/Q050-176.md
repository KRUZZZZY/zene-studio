# Q050-176 - Zoom history with back and forward (P1, completion, L13 customisation)

**Unit.** Move through several prior arrangement zoom levels and toggle between the current and previous extent
(WF-094 `B-WF-094`). On train 5 `arrangement.zoom_back` swapped the current zoom with the newest remembered one -
a one-entry toggle. The wheel and pinch already recorded their starting zoom, but the walk had no forward
direction and no way to visit several prior extents.

**Design.** The zoom walk is the browser's back / forward with a zoom toggle beside it:

- every zoom that changes the model remembers the extent it left behind and cuts what a forward step could
  return to (a new zoom breaks forward, like the browser);
- `arrangement.zoom_back` steps one extent back, queuing what it left behind;
- `arrangement.zoom_forward` steps one extent forward over what a back step went past;
- `arrangement.zoom_toggle` swaps the current extent with the one the zoom last left (a plain zoom, or the extent
  a back / forward step left behind), so pressing it twice flips between two extents. The swap reconciles the
  walk: the extent swapped to comes off the stack that holds it and the extent left behind queues in its place,
  so a back or forward step after the toggle never succeeds without a change (the round-1 review).

All three are interface state (`not_mutating`, no project write; the zoom itself is kept in the
`ui/songeditorzoom` setting as before). Like every keyboard zoom they keep the selection, else the playhead on
screen, in place on screen (0.5.0 theme 9).

## Format changes

None. The walk, the toggle and the forward queue are interface state in memory; nothing is added to the project
format, and old projects load and render exactly as before.

## Realtime

Nothing here touches the audio thread: the walk lives in `src/gui/ArrangerZoom.cpp` behind
`zoomingModel()->setValue` / `zoomAround` on the interface thread, and the command handlers run on the control
thread like the rest of `ControlCommandsArrangerView.cpp`. No allocation, locking, waiting or I/O linked to
audio; the module holds no audio state.

## Known limits

- The walk holds zoom extents only, not scroll positions: a restored extent re-anchors to the selection, else
  the playhead on screen (keyboard-zoom order), like every other zoom through the walk.
- The walk is per process (32 extents, as the one-entry walk before), not saved with the project.
- `arrangement.zoom_forward` after a NEW zoom is refused (the browser semantics); the toggle still works there,
  since a plain zoom leaves its previous extent behind for it.
