# Q050-123 - Take lanes and waveforms in the timeline

Unit: L, P1, L03 (take lanes / comping). Goal: take clips and their waveforms are visible in
timeline rows and in the comp strip, with the existing comp panel kept.

## What was built

- **One waveform painter** (`include/TakeWaveform.h`, `src/gui/TakeWaveform.cpp`): caches a
  `SampleThumbnail` per take buffer and draws it into the clip's own rectangle. It never invents a
  time mapping; the caller supplies the arranger or strip geometry, which is what keeps the
  waveform on the ruler.
- **Arrangement rows** (`include/TakeLaneRows.h`, `src/gui/tracks/TakeLaneRows.cpp`): a sample
  track with take lanes gets one row per lane over its content widget. Each clip is drawn at its
  own start/length on the arranger's existing `currentPosition`/`pixelsPerBar` mapping and its
  waveform is painted inside. The overlay takes no mouse input (`WA_TransparentForMouseEvents`), so
  the clip views underneath keep their gestures; it is hidden while the track has no lanes, which
  leaves ordinary tracks exactly as before. `TrackView` creates it for sample tracks; a 300 ms
  watch re-reads lanes, clips, scroll and zoom. Clip pointers are `QPointer`s, so an undo or a
  project load replacing the clips cannot leave a paint following a deleted clip.
- **Comp strip waveforms** (`src/gui/CompSwipeStrip.cpp`): the strip resolves its track's take
  clips and draws each waveform in its lane at the take's own position. The drawn span is the union
  of the comp's span and the takes' span, so a comp over part of a lane set no longer hides takes
  outside it. Active comp segments are painted translucently over the waveforms with an outline,
  so both the audio and the chosen segment are visible.
- **The existing panel is unchanged as the way in**: `TakeLanesPanel` still owns the strip,
  audition, Add lane, Comp, Play all takes and Consolidate; `strip()` is only a test accessor.
  No command, undo step, project field or saved display state was added. MIDI takes keep their
  existing clip presentation; no rendered MIDI waveform was added.

## Known limits

- The lane rows are a display overlay on the track's existing height; they do not change the
  track's saved height or lane data.
- MIDI take lanes keep their clip presentation; this unit adds waveforms for audio takes only.
- The rows follow a 300 ms watch plus repaints; a clip edit may take one tick of that watch to show.
