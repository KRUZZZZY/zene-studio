# Q050-087 - Device-chain patcher canvas

Queue unit (P1, L, feature, area L10, members `B-MX-109`, `R-workflow-22`, `R-workflow-144`): the patcher graph existed only
as commands (`patcher.get_state`, `patcher.set_wiring`) and a text form. Built on branch `feat/050-Q050-484-oc` together with
Q050-484, Q050-103 and Q050-071 (a feature batch; each unit has its own commits).

## What was built

- **The Chain Canvas** (`include/PatcherCanvas.h`, `src/gui/PatcherCanvas.cpp`; `PatcherGraph` in `include/PatcherGraph.h` /
  `src/gui/PatcherGraph.cpp`): a window that draws one track's or mixer channel's chain as its patcher graph - the chain's
  Input node, one node per device in chain order, ports, the cables in force (`effective_wiring` of `patcher.get_state`: the
  derived linear wiring or the authored one), and an OUT mark on the node the chain leaves through. Open it from a mixer
  channel's **Rack > Chain Canvas...** menu entry (one window per target).
- **Every edit is a registered command, one undo step, and the picture is re-read from `patcher.get_state` after each**, so
  what is drawn is what an agent reads:
  - **connect** - drag from a node's output port to another node's input port: `patcher.set_wiring` with the cables plus the new one;
  - **disconnect** - click a cable, press Delete (or right-click > Disconnect): `patcher.set_wiring` without it;
  - **second destination** - **Shift-click a cable** and drag to another node: a new cable from the *same source*, the original stays
    (a plain click only selects);
  - **reorder** - drag a node sideways, or Move Left / Move Right: `plugin.move`;
  - **add** - Add device... opens the device picker (`DevicePicker`), then `plugin.load` at the end of the chain;
  - **remove** - Remove / Delete on a node: `plugin.unload`;
  - **Use as Output** (node menu) and **Reset Wiring** (derived wiring again): `patcher.set_wiring`.
- **No new command** and no change to a command's class: `patcher.get_state` stays `not_mutating`, `patcher.set_wiring`
  `snapshot`, `plugin.load` `snapshot`, `plugin.move` and `plugin.unload` `true_inverse`. The menu entry carries
  `controlCommand = patcher.set_wiring` like the other registered actions.

## What the canvas can and cannot edit (the scope decision)

The patcher graph is live only for a chain whose devices have **no audio-ports model** (`EffectChain::rebuildRoutingGraph`
returns early otherwise), and the built-in devices this tree ships are audio-ports (`DefaultEffect`). So, honestly:

- on a chain the engine routes through its graph (legacy effects) the canvas edits the cables;
- on a chain of shipped devices it **shows the same cables read-only with the engine's reason** (the window's note, and
  `patcher.get_state`'s `editable.reason`), refuses a cable drag without changing anything, and add / reorder / remove still work
  through the device commands. Making audio-ports devices routable through the graph is an engine change this unit does not make.
- Also out of scope (they were not in the acceptance): saving authored wiring in the project (it is session state, `<fxchain>`
  carries none), a general node editor with generators or utility nodes, and touch gestures.
- Cables attach to chain **positions** (`effect:<index>`): after Move Left/Right the authored cables stay on the positions, not
  on the moved device. A chain on its derived wiring is unaffected.
- A wiring that would strand the output (for example cutting the only cable into a middle device of a linear chain) is refused
  by the engine, typed; the canvas shows the message and keeps the cable.

## Format and projects

No project-format change; authored wiring remains session state. Old projects open as before.

## Known limits

See the scope decision above. Only the Linux/offscreen run was made; the canvas uses plain `QPainter`/`QWidget` APIs available
in Qt 5.15 and 6.8 (no Qt-6-only API).
