## Known limits (unchanged, now stated in one place)

A Discrete clip keeps its per-tick step; a CubicHermite curve is approximated as one straight
segment per tick; a device that never reads `AutomatableModel::valueBuffer()` keeps the block's
single value; a clip in a pattern is refused; a block denser than the 32-knot ramp capacity is
refused the surplus knots and counts them; a transport jump inside a block is read with that
block's own ramp until the next block. `docs/KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md` §Sample-accurate automation
and `docs/SAMPLE-ACCURATE-AUTOMATION.md` carry the full account.
