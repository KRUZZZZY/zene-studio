# Q050-219 - Move arrangement sections across tracks

Unit: M, P2, L04. Goal: drag a named section in the ruler to move its material across tracks, with ripple or gap behaviour, as one undoable edit.
Built in one batch with Q050-255, which shares `include/ClipGroupEdits.h`.

## What was built

- **`section.move`** (`section`, `start`, optional `mode`). Every track is cut at the section's edges and what starts inside moves with it. The section keeps its id, name, colour and length.
- **Gap.** Only the section's material moves. A gap stays at the source, and what lies at the destination stays and may overlap.
- **Ripple.** The song is reordered: the material the section passes moves the other way by its length, closing the gap, with a third cut at the far edge of that stretch. Other sections wholly inside that stretch go with their material.
- **Ruler** (`ArrangerRulerLanes`). A hand cursor over a block, a closed hand and dashed drop preview while dragging, snapped (Alt for free). The release is one `section.move`. A press without a drag still selects (now on release) and a double-click still zooms.
- **Choosing the mode.** The drag follows the arranger's ripple mode (Navigate, or the lanes' menu "Moving a section"), and Shift at the drop picks the other behaviour for that drag. With no `mode`, the command follows the same setting.

## Commands

| Command | Class | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `section.move` | true_inverse | Checkpoints of every track and an action checkpoint for the section list, merged by the registry into one step |

Format: no change. Sections keep the optional `<sections>` element, so a project without it still loads with none. Realtime: everything runs on the UI thread, nothing on the audio path.

## Known limits

- A section's material means clips on the song's tracks. Markers, tempo, chords, loop bounds and the song's global automation track stay.
- A section only partly inside the passed stretch stays where it is, even though its material moves.
- A locked clip the move would touch refuses the whole move.
- A press on a section now selects on release rather than on press.
