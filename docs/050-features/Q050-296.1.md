# Q050-296.1 - A 5.1 or 7.1 channel's speakers through its devices

Type: completion (P2, size L), stage 1 of 4 of Q050-296. Member: R-mixing-11. Area: L08. Builds on
Q050-155.1/Q050-155.2 (discrete buses and device pins).

## What was built

- **A named speaker layout's devices see its speakers.** A 5.1 or 7.1 mixer channel's device chain runs on a
  wide bus carrying the layout's own channels L R C LFE Ls Rs (7.1 adds Lrs Rrs), not just the first pair
  (any speaker layout wider than the pair, LCR and quad included).
  `MixerChannel::processInserts` (`src/core/Mixer.cpp`) takes the surround path, `surround::processWideInserts`
  (`src/core/MixerSurroundMix.cpp`) maps pair 0 to the channel's own buffer and pair p to speakers 2p/2p+1.
- **Default pins change nothing.** A device with default pins still processes L/R only; every other speaker
  passes through the chain untouched, so an old project's 5.1 channel renders as before.
- **The pin matrix follows the layout.** `AudioPortsModel::growTrackChannels` widens a device's track channels to
  the host bus's speaker count, the Pin Connector's rows grow with it, and its **Channels** menu plus
  `port.set_pins` (the agent command) move a device onto a pair such as channels 5-6 (Ls/Rs) - one undo step.
- **Every speaker leaves on its own output.** When the channel's direct out or master sits on a device with
  enough outputs, speaker i leaves on output i.

## Commands, output and format changes

- New commands: none.
- Changed output: `port.get_state`'s `bus.format` names the channel's own named layout (`"5.1"` / `"7.1"`) and
  `bus.channels` counts its speakers.
- Format changes: none. Old projects load and a 5.1 channel's devices with default pins render as before.

## Realtime and threads

No allocation, free, lock, wait or I/O on the audio path. The insert pairs are grown in `ChannelSurround::setFormat`
on the control thread, under the engine's change lock; the allocation probe test proves zero allocations for the
wide master mix. TSan is not run locally; this is the race analysis.

## Known limits

- Post-fader inserts, send delay compensation, the mono/polarity/width/EQ stages and the meters still apply to L/R
  only.
- Post-fader inserts are out of scope for stage 1/4.
- A channel with a rack (two or more chains) still runs its chains on the pair only.
