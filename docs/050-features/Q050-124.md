# Q050-124 Freeze mixer buses

Branch `feat/050-Q050-124-oc` (cut from `050/m0-train-24`, `2a7fd9f9c7`). A mixer bus can be frozen: its output is
rendered once, and while the song plays the bus plays that take in place of its live inputs, at its own place in the
routing graph, until it is unfrozen.

## How it works

- **Where the take sits.** A bus's output is its routed inputs summed, then its trim, devices (chain or rack),
  hardware insert, VCA gain, strip output stages and pan - `MixerChannel::m_buffer` just before the fader. The render
  captures exactly that point and playback writes the take back at exactly that point, so the fader, the sends to
  other buses and the master, the sidechain taps, the post-fader inserts and the meters keep working live on the
  take (include/MixerBusFreeze.h).
- **Render.** `BounceInPlace::renderBus` (src/core/MixerBusFreeze.cpp) saves the session to a scratch copy and runs
  the shipped CLI render as a child process with `--capture-channel <stable id>` and `--float`: after the project
  loads, `busfreeze::armCapture` makes the child write that channel's pre-fader output instead of the master
  (`busfreeze::capture` in `MixerChannel::doProcessing`, `busfreeze::replaceOutput` in `Mixer::masterMix`). The whole
  song is rendered, so every track and channel upstream of the bus is in the take, and an upstream frozen bus plays
  its own take into it. The output is staged and installed with the bounce's own staged-output helpers. The session
  in this process is not modified.
- **Playback.** `MixerChannel::m_freeze` (`BusFreeze`) holds the take's path and the decoded `SampleBuffer` (control
  thread) plus a raw frame pointer, frame count and rate for the audio thread, written only under
  `AudioEngine::requestChangesGuard`; the old buffer is released on the control thread after the lock. Each period,
  `AudioEngine::renderStageNoteSetup` publishes where the period starts on the song timeline
  (`busfreeze::setPeriod`: two relaxed atomic stores; playing in Song mode, an export included). In
  `MixerChannel::doProcessing`, after the receive loop, `busfreeze::substitute` copies the take's frames for that
  period over `m_buffer` (zero-filling past the take's end) and the bus skips its trim and `processInserts()` (the
  trim-to-pan stages, moved unchanged out of `doProcessing`). It does nothing - the live chain runs as before - when
  the bus is not frozen, its audio is not loaded, the take's rate is not the engine's, the transport is stopped, or
  the period lies past the take's end.
- **Agent surface.** `freeze.bus {channel, out?}` and `freeze.unfreeze_bus {channel}`
  (src/core/ControlCommandsBusFreeze.cpp), addressed by the bus's stable `ch-<n>`. `bus.list` reads the state back
  (`frozen`, `frozen_audio`, `frozen_audio_ready`).
- **GUI.** The bus strip's context menu has **Freeze bus** / **Unfreeze bus** (src/gui/MixerBusFreezeMenu.cpp, added
  in `MixerChannelView::contextMenuEvent`), running the same two registered commands. A strip that is not a bus has
  neither.

## Commands

| id | class | inverse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `freeze.bus` | `true_inverse` | one action step: clear the take on the channel with this stable id (redo re-installs the same file) |
| `freeze.unfreeze_bus` | `true_inverse` | one action step: re-install the same take file (named inverse `control.undo`; `freeze.bus` would render a new one) |

`bus.list` (not_mutating) entries gain `frozen`, `frozen_audio`, `frozen_audio_ready`. Snapshot regenerated from a
live instance: 880 ids / 77 groups. A16 histogram: `rows=889 true_inverse=436 snapshot=42 irreversible=25
not_mutating=386` (docs/RELEASE-NOTES-v0.3.0-alpha.md). The render CLI gains `--capture-channel <id>` (the child of
`freeze.bus`; doc/zene.1).

## Format

Additive: a frozen bus's `<mixerchannel>` carries `frozenAudio="<absolute path of the take>"`. Absent means unfrozen
(reset on absence on every load), so a project without it - every older project - loads and renders exactly as
before, and an unfrozen bus saves byte-identically to before. The take is a 32-bit float WAV, by default
`<project>-bounces/ch-<n>-frozen-bus-<8 hex>.wav` beside the project (one new file per freeze, so a take an undo step
names is never overwritten).

## Known limits

- The take plays on the project's one tempo: `freeze.bus` is refused while the tempo map is on, and a tempo map
  turned on after freezing is not followed (unfreeze and freeze again).
- A loop that wraps in the middle of a period places at most that period's remainder from the old position (the take
  is read from the period's start frame).
- The take plays only at the rate it was rendered at; after a sample-rate change the bus plays live until it is frozen
  again.
- Tracks upstream of a frozen bus still run (their CPU is not saved yet) - their output is simply not used; tracks
  that ALSO route elsewhere keep doing so, which is correct.
- The render is blocking, like `freeze.track`'s synchronous contract; the render queue does not carry bus freezes.
- Found while building the socket fixture (not changed here, out of scope): headless, a track created BEFORE a mixer
  channel exists keeps playing to the master after `track.set_mixer_channel` to that channel - the reply names the
  new index but the track's channel model was ranged over the old channel count, and the saved file says `mixch="0"`.
  The transcript creates its channels first.
