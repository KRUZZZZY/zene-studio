# Voice and handle pools for render-path sample playback

Date: 2026-10-09  
Lane: `voice-pool`  
Base: `c405233f8`

## Problems confirmed

**5127 — metronome clicks.** `src/core/Metronome.cpp:31-40` calls `metronomeoptions::click()` while scheduling a render period. `src/core/MetronomeOptions.cpp:148-164` selects an already-decoded buffer in the usual case, but still allocates a `Sample` and `SamplePlayHandle` for every click; if options have not loaded it calls `SampleBuffer::fromFile()` there as well. `SamplePlayHandle` also creates its playback resampler and owned `AudioBusHandle` in its constructor. The settings-registration path normally calls `options()` first (`ProjectSectionRegistry.cpp:70`), but that does not remove the per-click allocations or make the fallback safe.

**5414 — frozen takes and pooled-handle lifetime.** `Track::playFrozenTake()` (`src/core/Track.cpp:1206-1272`) is reached from `SampleTrack::play()` and `InstrumentTrack::play()`. Each pass needs independent state and its own source window. A long take looping over a short timeline loop can have many passes alive together; the former eight-pass limit is not derived from the audio's lifetime. Pooled handles are constructed on the engine thread, but `SamplePlayHandle::affinityMatters()` (`include/SamplePlayHandle.h:69`) makes the render-stage completion sweep compare them with that construction thread. Finally, `SampleTrack::~SampleTrack()` removes sample handles but `InstrumentTrack::~InstrumentTrack()` does not remove frozen sample handles before the track dies, leaving a later pool retirement with a stale track pointer.

**4467 — SlicerT note state.** `plugins/SlicerT/SlicerT.cpp:78-116` allocates `Sample::PlaybackState` the first time `playNoteImpl()` sees a note. `NotePlayHandle::play()` invokes this plug-in hook while rendering; the state constructor creates an `AudioResampler`, whose `src_new()` call is in `src/core/AudioResampler.cpp:48-56`. The hook has no earlier plug-in initialization phase. `deleteNotePluginData()` at `SlicerT.cpp:140-144` currently frees that state when the note ends.

## Chosen design

Use one fixed-capacity `SamplePlayHandle` voice pool owned by `AudioEngine`, plus a fixed per-instance playback-state pool in SlicerT. The pool is deliberately for the metronome and frozen-take handles only; ordinary clip handles retain their existing construction and lifetime.

The engine voice pool has a hard maximum of **512** stable handle slots: **32 reserved for metronome clicks** and up to **480 for frozen takes**. Each slot contains a `Sample` and a `SamplePlayHandle` with its own `Sample::PlaybackState`; all slots use one engine-owned, no-effects `AudioBusHandle`. The common bus reserves space for all 512 handles on the control thread and is not exposed as a hardware port. Construct all slots on the engine/control thread. Two fixed ready queues keep the 32 click slots separate from the 480 frozen slots. The main-thread timer services retired slots; the audio thread only claims an already-ready slot. A track's logical active-pass allowance grows on the control thread from one to the derived requirement `ceil((takeEnd - max(takeStart, loopBegin)) / loopLength)` when the enabled loop intersects the take, capped at the 480 frozen slots. The backing handles remain preallocated and shared across tracks; no project change allocates on render. A pass refused by its per-track allowance or the global pool is counted and exposed by `control.ping` (`audio.sample_voice_overflow`); the metronome's 32 slots cannot be consumed by frozen takes.

Use fixed SPSC ready queues from the main thread to the render thread and a fixed SPSC render-retire queue in the reverse direction, sized to hold their full slot groups. Finished pooled handles are detached from the engine and bus lists, then enqueued without destruction. Control-side cancellation uses a separate bounded MPSC return queue because model removal can come from more than one control thread. The existing main-thread timer drains both return queues, resets handles, clears their shared sample references and makes each slot ready again. Queue occupancy across both return queues cannot exceed the number of pool slots: a slot is in exactly one of ready, active/staged, or retired states. Queue-full is an invariant failure counted in `control.ping` (`audio.sample_voice_queue_invariant_failures`), never a render-thread delete fallback.

Each pooled handle is constructed on the control thread with its slot's empty sample and the shared bus. Its pooled state explicitly opts out of construction-thread affinity, so any render callback or assigned engine worker can play and finish it; ordinary sample handles keep their existing affinity contract. Arming on render only copies a `shared_ptr` to the source buffer, sets POD window/amplification/offset fields, associates a track for removal, and starts from an already-reset playback state. Before a slot re-enters the ready queue, the control thread resets the resampler, clears its buffer view and sample reference, and clears its track association. Track teardown removes associated pooled handles under the engine change guard while the `Track` base state is alive; retirement clears the association before returning a slot, so no later service can dereference the destroyed track. The slot's source reference keeps audio storage alive if a frozen take is cleared while a voice is active; the final voice reference is released by the main-thread recycler, never on render.

For frozen takes, keep render-thread pass-start detection and an active-pass count. A pass starts on the first render call inside the frozen window, after a stop/seek/mute reset, or after transport wrap/re-entry while an older voice is still active. Each detected pass claims its own voice and snapshots `sourceIn` from that call's `TimePos`; existing voices continue to render to their own source end. The per-track allowance is recomputed on the control thread when the take or active timeline loop changes; the atomic allowance and active count are bounded by the global 480-slot frozen partition. An excess pass is skipped and included in the visible overflow counter. Keep the current mute, window, solo, and one-start-per-pass rules.

Each SlicerT instance owns **128** `Sample::PlaybackState` slots created with the instrument on its loader/control thread. A note claims one with a bounded scan and stores the stable slot address in `NotePlayHandle::m_pluginData`. Release resets the state's resampler/frame index/buffer view and marks the slot free; neither claiming nor releasing creates or destroys state on the audio thread. At 128 occupied slots, that note produces silence and increments a global `audio.slicert_state_overflow` counter in `control.ping`. This is the defined polyphony ceiling for this instrument instance; states are never shared between live notes.

## Invariants and realtime argument

| Object / operation | Owner and thread | Allocation and release | Capacity behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pooled sample handle, sample, resampler, shared bus | `AudioEngine` pool; constructed/refilled/reset/destroyed on engine/control thread; pooled handles render on any callback/worker thread | At most 512 handles and one bus, each off render; pooled handles are not deleted when a render finishes | Per-track frozen allowance grows to the take/loop overlap bound, at most 480; no ready slot or allowance: skip one click/pass and increment `audio.sample_voice_overflow`; reserved metronome slots remain available |
| Ready/retire queue entries | Fixed arrays owned by `AudioEngine` | Allocated with engine; render enqueue is a bounded SPSC index/value store; main timer dequeues and resets | Total queued slots cannot exceed 512; a violation increments the queue-invariant counter and leaves the handle owned, never deletes it on render |
| Frozen take buffer and track association | `Track` owns the frozen reference; each active voice holds a shared reference and a teardown-only track association | Loaded/frozen/cleared on control thread; teardown removes voices under the engine change guard; main-thread retirement clears the association and source reference | Clearing a take drops the track's reference; live voices finish from retained references; track destruction cancels associated voices before the base state dies |
| SlicerT playback states | One instrument instance owns 128 stable slots | Constructed with SlicerT off render; retained for the instance lifetime; resampler reset on slot release | Exhausted instance renders the new note as silence and increments `audio.slicert_state_overflow` |

The `Metronome::processTick()` and `Track::playFrozenTake()` render paths perform bounded queue operations, pointer checks, shared-reference increments whose backing source remains owned, atomic/POD field updates, and the existing engine handle enqueue. They do not call `new`, `delete`, `fromFile`, a Qt signal, a mutex, or a container growth path. Completion only enqueues a stable pointer; freeing, resampler reset, and shared-reference release happen in the main-thread pool service. SlicerT's play hook scans at most 128 slots and uses a preconstructed state; its release path resets and marks a slot without freeing memory. Track deletion holds the existing engine change lock while it removes active frozen voices, preventing a later callback from touching the dying object.

At capacities, the only audible difference is a counted skipped click/pass or a counted silent SlicerT note. Below capacity, sample choice, accent, gain, offset, source window, per-note resampler history, overlap, mute/solo and transport rules remain unchanged. A pooled handle's solo render and first render after reuse must compare bit-identically with a fresh handle for the same source window. Concurrent pooled voices sum on one bus before reaching the mixer, so compare that mix with the former per-handle buses using a maximum per-sample absolute error of `1e-6` and verify there is no wider audible change.

## Considered and rejected

- **Keep `new`/`delete` but warm the allocator:** does not remove render-path allocation or destruction and gives no capacity bound.
- **Load a metronome file as a fallback on first click:** keeps file I/O reachable from rendering; registration already loads all built-in buffers on the control thread.
- **One shared `PlaybackState` for SlicerT:** corrupts frame position, buffer view and resampler history when notes overlap.
- **Unbounded pools or growing vectors when full:** moves unbounded memory growth to the control path and gives no defined overload behaviour; pool growth is capped at 512 handles / 128 states per SlicerT instance.
- **Delete finished handles immediately on render:** destroys the bus/sample/resampler and may free the source buffer on the audio thread. Retirement keeps all reclamation on the control thread.
- **Reuse a frozen handle without resetting its window/state:** carries the old pass's resampler history and `sourceIn` into the next pass, so a mid-take start and overlapping passes render incorrectly.

## Verification

`FrozenTakeHandoffTest` exercises allocation-free scheduling, retirement from a thread other than pool construction, more than 32 completed clicks, independent source windows, a take longer than a short loop with more than eight simultaneous passes, track deletion mid-playback for both track types, the 480-global frozen limit, visible refusal counters, and the queue-invariant counter. Restoring the former allocating metronome path fails its allocation assertion (8 allocations versus 0); restoring the former frozen-pass allocations fails its assertion (7 versus 0). The pooled click and sample re-arm comparisons are bit-identical to fresh handles. The two-voice comparison against separate legacy buses stays within the stated `1e-6` maximum per-sample absolute error. `ControlRegistryTest` verifies the new `control.ping` counters.

`SlicerTPlayheadTest` compares a reused playback slot with a fresh slot bit-for-bit over many notes, checks instrument deletion while a slot is occupied, and covers overflow. Restoring the old note-hook `new`/`delete` path makes its allocation probe fail (1 allocation versus 0); the pool passes. `tests/rt-safety-sweep.py --check` reports zero fresh allocation/locking/growth hits for the declared paths and adds no allowlist entries.
